[GLOBAL EXECUTION RULES]
Real-world speed and normal human cadence; no slow motion, no time stretching, no speed ramp.
The camera observes the person. It never changes what the person is doing.
At an established observation position, allow brief foreground obstruction, edge framing, partial departure from the frame, and temporary loss of product readability. Never recenter, rush, or recover the subject or product for visibility; use only the already established camera movement.
Do not add any action, object, event, or product beat that is not written in the timeline.
Camera observes life; it does not guarantee full-body, centered, or continuously readable presentation.
The camera may discover the product, but never gives it its own frame: no ankle-level or shoe-level framing, prolonged lower-body-only crop, upward shoe reveal tilt, or product-motivated lowering of the camera.
Ordinary observations stay at believable human-observer height unless real scene geometry requires otherwise. Incidental partial visibility is allowed; a deliberate footwear crop is not.
A mandatory event must be legible, but complete action coverage is not required. The camera may see the beginning or consequence, and the next visible state may already show completion.
Walking routes use the existing scene geometry rather than a cleared center runway; the protagonist may enter off-center, cross the frame diagonally, drift toward one side, and leave the optical center without any camera correction.
Moment windows are timing metadata, not performance windows: once an event is established, the remaining time flows into the existing action without extending or repeating that event.
A Moment boundary does not rebuild the camera-to-subject relation; inside one continuous Take the camera keeps its position, framing, and distance.
Existing environment activity may legally cross or block part of the view; do not avoid, re-frame, or recover for it.
Emotion never creates a new action. Quiet, settled, unhurried, relaxed, after work, pause, slows, and stays remain ordinary body states: they do not become a performed gesture, an added visible response, an audible reaction, or an emotional pause. Default emotion execution is neutral, internally occupied, and unperformed.
Sound is natural world sound only: no music, no foreground dialogue, no voiceover, no narration.
Use only the sounds written in the timeline; do not add any other object, door, or surface sound.
Do not advance past a moment's described end state; a timeline boundary never requires a physical stop.
A Moment boundary is timing and state evidence, not a command to stop or restart the person's movement.
