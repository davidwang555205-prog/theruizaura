[GLOBAL EXECUTION RULES]
Real-world speed and normal human cadence; no slow motion, no time stretching, no speed ramp.
The camera observes the person. It never changes what the person is doing.
At an established observation position, allow brief foreground obstruction, edge framing, partial departure from the frame, and temporary loss of product readability. Never recenter, rush, or recover the subject or product for visibility; use only the already established camera movement.
Do not add any action, object, event, or product beat that is not written in the timeline.
Emotion never creates a new action. Quiet, settled, unhurried, relaxed, after work, pause, slows, and stays remain ordinary body states: they do not become a performed gesture, an added visible response, an audible reaction, or an emotional pause. Default emotion execution is neutral, internally occupied, and unperformed.
Sound is natural world sound only: no music, no foreground dialogue, no voiceover, no narration.
Use only the sounds written in the timeline; do not add any other object, door, or surface sound.
Do not advance past a moment's described end state; a timeline boundary never requires a physical stop.
A Moment boundary is timing and state evidence, not a command to stop or restart the person's movement.
