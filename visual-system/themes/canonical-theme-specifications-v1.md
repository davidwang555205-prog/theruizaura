# THERUIZ AURA｜Canonical Theme Specifications v1

These specifications are model-neutral. They define purpose, person state, action logic, scene evidence, product boundaries, variation axes and QA requirements. They are not Image2 prompts.

All formal specifications inherit:

- Quiet Warm Luxury as observable warmth, restraint, tactile materials and believable space;
- an approximate 25–46 visual age range without SKU-based routing;
- the rule that the person is not a product prop;
- Product Truth from current task uploads only;
- brand anchors as abstract language references, never product evidence;
- no mechanical copying of a reference anchor;
- no automatic promotion of generated results to frozen anchors.

The eight specifications are grouped into Lifestyle, Official Studio, Product Presentation, Material & Craft, and Supporting Scene Library. The supporting library supplies location evidence but cannot replace a formal theme purpose.

## Merge and retirement policy

No source theme is currently merged or retired. Overlaps between daily lifestyle, outfit solution, seasonal study, brand point of view, and material/development themes remain explicit until Phase 3-D produces controlled old/new evidence. This preserves rollback and avoids deleting historical runtime assets.

## Runtime invariants

1. Every formal theme has a stable `theme_id` and version.
2. Product Truth is an input boundary, never a theme-derived value.
3. A scene name cannot substitute for content purpose.
4. A supporting scene entry cannot compile without an owning canonical theme.
5. Theme status `supporting_library` is not a formal delivery theme by itself.
