# Phase 2 Visual Review Form

For each generated image, score applicable items from 1 to 5. Mark N/A only for categories excluded by the case human/product role.

## Required fields

- Case ID and image ID
- Version: A or B
- Product Truth: silhouette, toe box, outsole, panels, material, color, laces/tongue, proportion
- Physical Integrity: foot/shoe relation, garment separation, grounding, floating, clipping, duplicate shoe, anatomy
- Composition: product role, subject relation, shot scale, negative space, readability, commercial usability
- Human/Action when applicable: naturalness, stability, realism, face, clothing/action relation, product attention balance
- Brand: Quiet Warm Luxury, restraint, warmth, mature urban feeling, material realism, low-saturation order, AI polish
- Artifact tags: plastic CGI, generic catalog, over-styled, artificial pose, over-smoothed skin, clutter, fake material, excessive symmetry, prompt literalism, deformation

## Case conclusion

Choose exactly one: B_CLEARLY_BETTER, B_SLIGHTLY_BETTER, EQUIVALENT, A_SLIGHTLY_BETTER, A_CLEARLY_BETTER, BOTH_FAIL, INVALID_TEST. Record evidence, whether Product Truth declined, suspected adjective competition, suspected emphasis weakening, optimization recommendation, and target module.
