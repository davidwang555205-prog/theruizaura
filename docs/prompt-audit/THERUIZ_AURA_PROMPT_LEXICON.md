# THERUIZ AURA Prompt Lexicon

This is a responsibility map, not a ban list. A word is retained when it carries a distinct visual job.

| Responsibility | Preferred vocabulary | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| brand_tone | quiet warm luxury, restrained, refined | Brand attitude; do not use as product-structure language. |
| emotion / human_state | calm, relaxed, mature, unperformed | Person state; do not repeat as scene or palette padding. |
| palette | warm neutrals, low-saturation, muted blue, soft stone | Color direction only; never infer uploaded product color. |
| light | sourced light, natural falloff, soft local shadows | Light behavior; avoid stacking soft/gentle/quiet without a light action. |
| texture / material | tactile, dense matte surface, believable fabric weight | Surface response; product material names require confirmed Product Truth. |
| spatial_order | calm negative space, believable imbalance, lived-in | Spatial composition; not a substitute for camera or product role. |
| composition / camera | full-figure, three-quarter, natural scale, camera profile | Framing and perspective; image type remains authoritative. |
| styling | low-logo tailoring, exact hems, restrained accessory | Garment and accessory behavior; not product truth. |
| product_truth | uploaded reference, toe box, panels, outsole, laces, tongue | Hard product evidence; never generated from image role alone. |
| physical_integrity | foot seated, grounded contact, no clipping or fusion | Hard physical constraint. |
| negative_constraint | floating feet, distorted outsole, duplicated shoe details | Keep only high-probability or high-loss failures. |
| seasonal_semantics | breathable freshness, warm interior, cool early light | Season controls climate and light, not product facts. |

## Near-synonym handling

- Use one primary brand-tone phrase per block; do not stack quiet, calm, soft, gentle, refined, restrained, and understated without separate visual duties.
- Keep `relaxed` for human state, `tactile` for surface response, and `calm negative space` for composition.
- `natural` must identify what is natural: light, pose, material, or camera moment.
- `subtle` belongs to product presence or a deliberately limited detail, not every sentence.

## Boundary rules

THERUIZ AURA words are brand-specific. Audit mechanics, rule IDs, priorities, conflict detection, and role exclusivity are platform-general. Image2 rendering order and reference wording remain adapter-specific.
